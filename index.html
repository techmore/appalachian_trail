Camp the Appalachian Trail Near 18947

Zip code 18947 = Pipersville, Bucks County, Pennsylvania. You're sitting in one of the best spots on the East Coast for a quick Appalachian Trail adventure. Within a 5–40 minute drive you'll find AT access points, riverside campgrounds, state parks, county parks, and the Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area — all perfect for a couple's one-night getaway.

This site is built for newbies: no complicated backpacking gear lists, no multi-day logistics. Just straightforward spots where you can park, pitch a tent, build a fire, and get a taste of the AT lifestyle.
