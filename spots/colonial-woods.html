--- title: "Colonial Woods Family Camping Resort" description: "Guide to Colonial Woods Family Camping Resort near Pipersville, PA — a full-service family camping resort with pool, mini-golf, and river access." --- # Colonial Woods Family Camping Resort

545 Lonely Cottage Drive, Upper Black Eddy, PA 18972

~20 minutes from Pipersville (18947)

Colonial Woods is a full-service family camping resort with over 200 campsites, cabins, a heated pool, mini-golf, and direct Delaware River access. It's a great option if you want amenities and activities along with your camping experience — or if you're tent-camping but want hot showers and a pool.

Why Go

  • Heated pool, kiddie splash pool, and spa
  • Mini-golf course
  • Fishing pond + Rapp Creek
  • Treasure Island Outfitters — island camping experience
  • Full bathhouses with hot showers
  • Laundry facilities
  • Dump station
  • Scheduled family activities and events all season
  • Nearby: Bucks County River Tube Company
