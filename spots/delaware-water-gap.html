--- title: "Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area" description: "Guide to camping and hiking at Delaware Water Gap NRA near Pipersville, PA — including primitive river sites, waterfalls, and Appalachian Trail access." --- # Delaware Water Gap NRA

Bushkill, PA ~ 30 minutes from Pipersville (18947)

No entrance fee

The Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area spans 70,000 acres along the Delaware River in Pennsylvania and New Jersey. It's the crown jewel of outdoor recreation near 18947 — with 40 miles of river, 100+ miles of trails (including AT sections), waterfalls, and primitive riverside camping.

Camping Options

Primitive River Campsites ($16/night)

  • 62 primitive sites along the Delaware River, accessible by boat or on foot
  • Zonal reservation system via Recreation.gov
  • Fire rings, primitive toilets at some sites
  • Must pack in water and supplies
  • Best for couples who want a真正的 "in nature" experience

Alosa Campsites ($16/night)

  • Walk-in/bike-in sites near Bushkill
  • Reserve on Recreation.gov

Group Campgrounds ($100/night)

  • Valley View Group Campground — riverside, 5 sites
  • Rivers Bend Group Campground — 4 sites
  • Best for 2+ couples together

AT Access Points Near Here

  • Dingmans Ferry Trailhead — AT crosses here, parking available
  • Bushkill Access — AT parking lot on US 209
  • Milford Beach — northern trail access

Newbie Tips

  • No entrance fee — just show up
  • Cell service is spotty to nonexistent in many areas — download maps ahead of time
  • Bear canisters or bear bags are required at primitive sites
  • No alcohol at river campsites
  • Bring bug spray — ticks and mosquitoes are abundant spring through fall
  • Waterfalls are best after rain; swimming holes are popular in summer
