Camp the Appalachian Trail Near 18947

Zip code 18947 = Pipersville, Bucks County, Pennsylvania. You're sitting in one of the best spots on the East Coast for a quick Appalachian Trail adventure. Within a 30–45 minute drive you'll find AT access points, riverside campgrounds, state parks, and the Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area — all perfect for a couple's one-night getaway.

This site is built for newbies: no complicated backpacking gear lists, no multi-day logistics. Just straightforward spots where you can park, pitch a tent, build a fire, and get a taste of the AT lifestyle.

Top Picks for a 1-Night Couples Trip

Tohickon Valley County Park

~5 min from 18947

Closest camping to Pipersville. 22 tent/trailer sites, seasonal pool, hiking trails connecting to Ralph Stover / High Rocks. Very beginner-friendly.

Cost: ~$15-25/night tent site

Full Guide

Delaware Water Gap NRA

~30 min from 18947

40 miles of river, waterfalls, AT access, and primitive riverside camping. Free entrance. The most scenic option within striking distance.

Cost: $16/night primitive sites

Full Guide

Ralph Stover / High Rocks

~5 min from 18947

45-acre state park with High Rocks overlook, rock climbing, and Tohickon Creek. Day-use only — camp at adjacent Tohickon Valley.

Cost: Free day-use
