--- title: "Tohickon Valley County Park" description: "Everything you need to know about camping at Tohickon Valley County Park near Pipersville, PA — rates, amenities, reservations, and tips for couples." --- # Tohickon Valley County Park

127 Cafferty Road, Point Pleasant, PA 18950

~5 minutes from Pipersville (18947)

Our #1 pick for first-time campers. Tohickon Valley County Park is a 612-acre park operated by Bucks County. It has 22 individual tent/trailer sites, 2 group camping areas, and 4 cabins — all within walking distance of the High Rocks overlook and connected trails to Ralph Stover State Park.

Why It's Great for Newbies

  • Closest option to 18947 — 5 minutes from Pipersville
  • Drive-up camping — park right at your site, no hiking with gear
  • Hot showers and flush toilets
  • Seasonal swimming pool (summer)
  • Hiking trails connecting to High Rocks and Ralph Stover
  • Fire rings and picnic tables at every site
  • Year-round availability (tent sites 9-22 closed Nov 15–Apr 15)

Costs (Ballpark)

ItemCost
Tent site (per night)$15-25
Cabin rental$40-60/night
Group camping$50-100/night
Firewood (on site)$5-8/bundle

Reservations

Book through Bucks County Parks & Recreation online reservation system at Rec1 or call 215-757-0571.

Things to Do

  • Hike to High Rocks — a 200-foot sheer cliff overlook of Tohickon Creek gorge (10-min walk from campsites)
  • Swim at the seasonal pool (Memorial Day–Labor Day)
  • Fish in Tohickon Creek
  • Whitewater kayaking — seasonal water releases attract paddlers
  • Mountain biking on park trails
  • Visit Ralph Stover State Park — connected by trail, day-use only (picnicking, climbing)

Rules to Know

  • No alcohol allowed (county park rule)
  • No pets at tent sites (check cabin policy)
  • Quiet hours: 10 PM – 7 AM
  • RV length limit: 25ft, trailer limit: 18ft
  • Carry-in / carry-out trash — no dumpsters
