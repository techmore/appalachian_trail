--- title: "Tinicum Park" description: "Guide to Tinicum Park campground near Pipersville, PA — small riverside camping along the Delaware River with disc golf and hiking." --- # Tinicum Park

462 River Road, Erwinna, PA 18920

~15 minutes from Pipersville (18947)

Tinicum Park is a 126-acre Bucks County park along the Delaware River with a small, quiet campground. It has 8 family campsites and a group camping area, plus access to the Delaware Canal towpath, disc golf, and the river itself. It's a hidden gem — rarely crowded, peaceful, and scenic.

Why Go

  • Small campground — usually quiet and uncrowded
  • Directly across from the Delaware River
  • Disc golf course on-site
  • Access to Delaware Canal State Park towpath (biking/walking)
  • Historic park with polo field (Saturday polo matches in season)
  • Good fishing access

Amenities

  • Fire rings
  • Picnic tables
  • Restrooms (basic, no showers)
  • Playground
  • Hiking trails
  • Disc golf
  • Boat launch (across River Rd)

Rules

  • No alcohol (Bucks County park)
  • No pets at tent sites
  • Call ahead to reserve — walk-ins sometimes accommodated
  • Carry-in/carry-out trash
