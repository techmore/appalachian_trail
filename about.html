--- title: "About This Site" description: "About the AT Near 18947 camping guide for couples." --- # About This Site

This site was built as a resource for couples (or anyone!) looking to dip their toes into camping near the Appalachian Trail without a huge commitment. The focus is the area around Pipersville, PA (zip code 18947) — a fantastic location for quick overnight trips.

Why 18947?

Pipersville sits in central Bucks County, PA — within 30 minutes of Delaware Water Gap, multiple AT access points, and some of the best state/county parks in the region. It's an ideal base camp for exploring the AT and nearby camping spots.
