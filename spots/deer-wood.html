--- title: "Deer Wood Campground" description: "Guide to Deer Wood Campground in Pipersville, PA — the closest camping to 18947, primitive tent sites in walking distance to High Rocks." --- # Deer Wood Campground

171 Cafferty Road, Pipersville, PA 18947

~2 minutes from central 18947 — literally in Pipersville!

Deer Wood Campground is a small, rustic campground operated by Bucks County — and it's the closest possible camping to zip code 18947. It's right on Cafferty Road, adjacent to Tohickon Valley Park and within walking distance of the High Rocks overlook.

This is a no-frills, back-to-basics camping experience. Each site has a fire grate and picnic table, but no hookups or modern amenities beyond basic restrooms. Perfect if you just want to pitch a tent, build a fire, and sleep under the stars without driving anywhere.

Why Go

  • Closest camping to 18947 — 2 minutes from downtown Pipersville
  • Walk to High Rocks overlook (5-10 min)
  • Very affordable — among the cheapest options around
  • Quiet and secluded despite being close to town
  • Adjacent to Tohickon Valley trails

Amenities

  • Fire rings with grates
  • Picnic tables
  • Basic restrooms (lit, with outlets)
  • No showers
  • No electric hookups
  • No dump station

Rules

  • No alcohol (Bucks County park rule)
  • No pets at tent sites
  • Quiet hours: 10 PM – 7 AM
  • Carry-in/carry-out trash
