--- title: "Nockamixon State Park" description: "Guide to Nockamixon State Park near Pipersville, PA — modern cabin rentals on 1,450-acre Lake Nockamixon with hiking, boating, and swimming." --- # Nockamixon State Park

1542 Mountain View Drive, Quakertown, PA 18951

~15 minutes from Pipersville (18947)

Nockamixon State Park spans 5,286 acres around 1,450-acre Lake Nockamixon — the third-largest lake in Pennsylvania. While it does not offer tent or RV camping, it has 10 modern cabins with full kitchens, bathrooms, and heat. A great "glamping" option for couples who want nature without sleeping on the ground.

Why Go

  • Modern cabins with kitchens, bathrooms, and heat — no tent required
  • 1,450-acre lake for boating, fishing, swimming
  • Hiking and mountain biking trails
  • Disc golf course (loaner discs available at park office)
  • Playground and swimming pool (seasonal)
  • Pet-friendly cabins available (cabins #1, 2, 3, 6)

Cabin Info

  • 10 modern cabins, 2-bedroom (sleeps 6) or 3-bedroom (sleeps 8)
  • Kitchen with refrigerator, stove, microwave
  • Living area with furniture
  • Private toilet and shower
  • Fire ring, grill, and picnic table outside
  • Bring your own: linens, towels, cookware, tableware
  • No air conditioning — bring a fan in summer

Activities

  • Boating (3 ramps, rentals available)
  • Fishing (walleye, bass, perch, catfish)
  • Swimming pool (not lake — seasonal)
  • Hiking (trails around the lake)
  • Mountain biking
  • Disc golf (18 holes)
  • Picnicking
  • Cross-country skiing (winter)
