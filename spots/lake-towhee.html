--- title: "Lake Towhee County Park" description: "Guide to Lake Towhee County Park near Pipersville, PA — a quiet, small county park campground with 13 sites on a 50-acre lake." --- # Lake Towhee County Park

1489 Old Bethlehem Road, Perkasie, PA 18944

~20 minutes from Pipersville (18947)

Lake Towhee is a small, quiet Bucks County park centered around a 50-acre lake. The campground has only 13 sites, making it one of the most peaceful options near 18947. Each site has a gravel pad, fire ring, and picnic table. Perfect if you want to get away from crowds.

Why Go

  • Small campground (13 sites) — very quiet
  • 50-acre lake for fishing and non-motorized boating
  • Hiking trails around the lake
  • Swimming (seasonal)
  • Playground
  • Good birdwatching and wildlife
  • Rarely fully booked
