--- title: "Ralph Stover State Park & High Rocks" description: "Guide to Ralph Stover State Park and High Rocks near Pipersville, PA — day hiking, rock climbing, and Tohickon Creek views." --- # Ralph Stover State Park & High Rocks

5998 State Park Road, Pipersville, PA 18947

~5 minutes from the heart of 18947

Ralph Stover State Park is a small (45-acre) state park that packs a big punch. The main attraction is High Rocks, a 200-foot sheer cliff overlooking Tohickon Creek gorge. It's a popular spot for rock climbing, hiking, picnicking, and photography.

Important: This is a day-use only park. No camping. But it's directly adjacent to Tohickon Valley County Park, so you can hike here from your campsite.

Why Visit

  • Stunning 200-ft cliff overlook — incredible sunset views
  • Easy hiking trails (1-3 miles) perfect for beginners
  • Rock climbing routes (bring your own gear)
  • Tohickon Creek — popular for whitewater kayaking during water releases
  • Free admission
  • Connected by trail to Tohickon Valley Park campsites

Recommended Itinerary

  1. Arrive at Tohickon Valley around 2-3 PM, set up camp
  2. Hike to High Rocks via the connecting trail (5-10 min walk from campsites)
  3. Watch sunset from the overlook — bring a blanket and snacks
  4. Walk back to camp before dark, build a fire
  5. Next morning: explore more trails or visit the nearby Delaware Canal
