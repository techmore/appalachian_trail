Camping Spots Near 18947

All of these spots are within a 30–45 minute drive of Pipersville, PA. Each is doable for a Friday-after-work departure and a Saturday return.

Tohickon Valley County Park

Point Pleasant, PA ~ 5 min from 18947

Tent camping, cabins, group sites. Hot showers, seasonal pool, trails connecting to High Rocks. Our top recommendation for absolute beginners.

Delaware Water Gap NRA

Bushkill / Dingmans Ferry, PA ~ 30 min from 18947

Primitive riverside and island camping. AT access, waterfalls, kayaking. Free entry, $16/night primitive sites via Recreation.gov.

Read More

Ralph Stover State Park / High Rocks

Pipersville, PA ~ 5 min from 18947

Day-use area with stunning 200-ft cliff overlook. Great afternoon hike before heading to your campsite at Tohickon Valley next door.

Read More

Dingmans Campground

Dingmans Ferry, PA ~ 35 min from 18947

134-site rustic campground right on the Delaware River. Tent and RV sites, general store, river access. No WiFi — truly unplug.

Cost: ~$35-45/night

KOA Delaware Water Gap / Pocono Mountain

East Stroudsburg, PA ~ 40 min from 18947

Full-service KOA with tent sites, cabins, pool, and amenities. Good option if you want a "glamping" feel or need to rent gear.

Cost: ~$45-75/night
