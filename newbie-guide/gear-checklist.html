--- title: "Gear Checklist for 1-Night Camping" description: "Complete gear checklist for a one-night car camping trip near the Appalachian Trail — with budget tips for beginners." --- # Gear Checklist: 1-Night Camping Trip

For a drive-up campsite (like Tohickon Valley), you don't need fancy backpacking gear. Here's exactly what you need for a comfortable one-night stay as a couple.

Clothing (Per Person)

  • Hiking clothes — shorts, t-shirt, long pants for evening
  • Rain jacket — even if forecast is clear
  • Warm layer — fleece or hoodie for evening (temps drop fast)
  • Extra socks — two pairs minimum (wool or synthetic)
  • Hat + gloves — yes, even in summer nights can be cool
  • Camp shoes / sandals — let your feet breathe at camp
  • Swimwear — if there's a pool or swimming hole

Toiletries & Safety

  • Toothbrush + toothpaste
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Biodegradable soap
  • First aid kit (bandaids, antiseptic, blister care, ibuprofen)
  • Sunscreen
  • Bug spray (DEET or picaridin) — essential in PA woods
  • Tick key / fine tweezers
  • Toilet paper + ziploc for pack-out (even if toilets exist)

Nice-to-Haves

  • Camp pillow or inflatable pillow
  • Deck of cards / board game
  • Bluetooth speaker (be respectful of quiet hours)
  • Camera / binoculars
  • Hammock (great for afternoon relaxing between trees)
  • Tarp (for rain cover or shade)
  • Extra blanket for stargazing
